GoalKick v0 (rung 5, Option A: mission layer only) -- evaluation summary, 2026-11-14
========================================================================================
Brief: microduck-goalkick-brief.md. Dillon chose Option A (frozen policies, GoalKick as a
supervisor feature). Sim only. Frictionless ball kept (Dillon: friction = separate item).

Stack (all frozen, unchanged): VelStand round3 3999 -> PrecisionStep v2 2500 -> BallKick v2
(right 1250 / left 500) with kick_mission.py --calib v2 (right 0 mm / -6.7 deg, left 0 mm /
+12.2 deg). No new constants.

Goal: goal.xml, 210 mm clear opening = 3 ball widths (Dillon), posts 20 mm dia x 120 mm,
full collision, no crossbar; mocap body placed per mission D m past the ball on the kick line.
Scoring (scripts/soccer_referee.py, pure logic, 10 unit tests):
  goal      ball centre crosses the goal line between the posts' inner faces, no post contact
  post      any ball-post contact (own line; "in" = it still crossed between the posts)
  miss      crosses the line extension outside the posts, no contact
  no_cross  never reaches the line within 10 s of the kick swap-in
  lateral   signed ball-centre offset at the line, + = left of centre as the kicker sees it.
            A clean goal needs |lateral| < 70 mm (105 mm half-opening - 35 mm ball radius).

3. FINDINGS (reported, not acted on)
- Every off-target kick hit a post; none went wide. 210 mm sits right at the kick's accuracy
  limit, so goals / posts both show up and the right foot's wider spread is visible.
- Right foot lands 12-17 mm right of centre at every distance (about -1 deg on top of its
  --calib v2 aim). Not re-calibrated: a separate measured decision for Dillon.
- Frictionless ball: it rolls on at ~0.17-0.25 m/s after a kick and never stops, so distance
  only adds lateral spread and time; nothing falls short in sim. On real floors it would.
- The 1.1 m stretch line is "in range" only because of that.

4. RENDER BUG FOUND AND FIXED (viewer only, physics untouched)
Goals parked at +-50 m inflated model.stat.extent 0.415 -> 100 m; MuJoCo scales the shadow map
and near/far clip by it -> blocky shadows + black horizon lines. An XML <statistic> pin
(652ecaaa) was overridden by BAM's mj_setConst; setup_goal() now restores extent/center
(cafebe8e). Verified through the real KickSim model; goal missions byte-identical before/after.
