     (the duck reports a ball whose stance spot is off the geofence, e.g. pinned on a post or in a corner).
  2. OUT = the ball touches a board (boards stand on the boundary lines), or its centre is past a line outside the mouth
     (a ball the duck pushed through a board's soft contact -- seen once).
  3. ADVANCE KICKS / TAP-INS aim at the goal's FIELD-MAP spot through the estimated pose. The camera finds the 20 mm posts
     only to ~2.5-3 m (ranging 2.5 m: 15/15, fit err <= 28 mm; 3.5 m: 5/15; 4 m: 4/15) on a 6 m field; and below ~0.9 m
     the posts overflow the frame. Between 0.6 and 2.5 m (ball->goal) the Stage 4 camera path is unchanged: goal seen at
     the pre-stance look, heading from the perceived goal, not seen after the sweep = goal_not_found. SCAN needs the ball only.
  4. ROUTINE LANDMARK FIXES (beyond the brief's fall trigger) -- standing in WATCH after each kick:
       full fix (position + heading) from a fixed goal at 0.9-1.3 m (facing) / <= 2.0 m (position);
       heading-only fix from the BEARING to a fixed goal through its map spot, <= 3 m.
     Why: odometry + IMU drift grows without bound over 240 s. Control without them (B_right_odom_noroutine below):
     end pose error median 237 mm (max 961); 6/30 runs ended goal_not_found -- every one with 10-18 deg of heading drift,
     which pushes the target goal outside the 0.6 m association gate.
     Gates: sightings only upright + still, >= 0.9 m; a fix may not turn the heading > 10 deg (4 agreeing looks at 0.95 m once
     put it 36 deg off) nor move a routine position > 0.5 m.
