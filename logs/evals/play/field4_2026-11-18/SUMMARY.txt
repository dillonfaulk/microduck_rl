WS1 THE FIELD (field4.xml, scene_field4.xml; play.py --field 4 is the default; --field 6 keeps the old board field)
  4 m x 4 m, lines at x, y = +-2.0; goals locked at (+-2, 0), 210 mm opening, 20 x 120 mm white posts (unchanged).
  Boundary wall: VISUAL ONLY (transparent blue, no collision), geom group 5 = not drawn by the duck's camera, not seen by the
  ToF (tof.py now casts groups 0-4 only); the viewer shows it.
  Corner posts: one centred on each corner, 75 mm diameter x 0.40 m tall, matte blue, full collision (= 3-inch PVC pipe,
  painted, weighted base). Blue so neither the white-post goal detector nor the orange-ball detector fires on it.
  Geofence (the duck's field): the lines moved OUT 0.25 m (+-2.25) -- a player may stand just over a line to play a ball near
  it. Unplayable = stance spot outside the geofence. Ball respawns 0.4 m inside the lines, >= 0.6 m from the duck.
  OUT (carried over): the WHOLE ball over a line outside the mouth (soccer rule); a GOAL = the centre crossing a goal line
  between the posts. The wall stops nothing.
  RETRIEVAL + RESPAWN, the precedence rule:
    1. The out call always fires and is counted. It opens a RETRIEVAL WINDOW of 45 s.
    2. In the window the duck leaves the field (geofence widens to the lines + 1.45 m) and kicks the ball back toward the
       centre spot (a map-aimed kick). The whole ball back inside a line = RETRIEVED: play on, same ball, no respawn.
    3. RESPAWN when the window expires, or at once if the ball is more than 1.0 m beyond a line, or if the duck reports the
       ball unplayable. No goal counts while the ball is out. A call during a kick waits for the kick to finish.
    4. A ball the duck estimates on/over a line is not reported unplayable for 3 s (the referee's out call has priority).
  ToF / mapd model, stated: the field's ToF signature is the 8 posts and the floor. Measured: a corner post returns 9/64 zones
  at 0.5 m, 4 at 1.0 m, 0 beyond 1.5 m; a goal post 0-3 zones. The shim and mapd are unmodified; mapd sees floor + posts.
  New routing rule (found on this field): routes detour around the 8 map posts (0.2 m clearance) -- a duck coming back in
  from behind a goal stood ON a goal post for 150 s stepping in place while its odometry walked 0.9 m.
  Map-aimed kicks aim 0.3 m INTO the goal (a ball resting on the line made ball->line-centre a 2 cm vector) and a goal seen
  more than 50 deg edge-on (corner balls) is map-aimed too (the two posts overlap; ranging only ever covered +-35 deg).

  ROOT CAUSE of the 42 deg stall (exact replay of 6 m LOOK run 5, state dumped at 60 s): pitched 43 deg nose-down (roll -10),
  trunk 0.10 m; neck_pitch -110 deg from HOME, head_yaw -59; the jaw in contact with the right ankle, the right leg, the left
  hip and the floor -- the head is folded under and jammed against its own legs. VelStand keeps commanding neck_pitch -3.83 rad
  (far past the joint range), pressing the beak into the floor: beak + two feet = a stable tripod. Zero command, head-neutral,
  walk/turn commands and forward/sideways 0.3 m/s pushes leave it stuck (tilt 43 deg after 10-20 s); a 0.3 m/s push BACKWARD
  frees it (tilt 1.7 deg). A self-entangled fixed point of the get-up behaviour: a TRAINING item (e.g. neck action limits /
  head-under-body states in StandUp/VelStand), not something the supervisor can command its way out of.
