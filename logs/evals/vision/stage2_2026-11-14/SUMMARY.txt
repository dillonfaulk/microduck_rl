B. WHAT WAS BUILT (2.2-2.6). Suite: 332 passed, 1 skipped; 13 new tests in tests/test_rung6_stage2.py.
   - sim/tof.py: TofFlaws switches, default ON: axial_range, latency_s 0.060, pitch_offset_deg 3.0,
     dropout (table above).
     - Uses mj_multiRay; TofFlaws.none() is bit-identical to the old mj_ray sensor (tested).
     - frame_at(data, t) applies the latency.
     - The body server now serves frame_at, so the twin's tofd gets the flawed sensor that mapd's
       defaults assume.
   - sim/board_profiles.py: face-board-v1 (PRIMARY: GC2093 85.2x54.7 upright, VL53L8CX) and zero3
     (IMX219 62.2x37.4 rotate 90, VL53L5CX), with family-solve intrinsics and distortion.
     - ToF parameters do not differ between the chips in our model: no VL53L8CX measurement exists,
       so the L8CX carries the L5CX's values, stated in the profile.
   - sim/camera.py: apply_profile() mounts head_camera off the tof site (upright or rotate-90) and
     sets fovy from the solve. Renders are checked in camera/*.png. The legacy twin camera stays the
     body server's default, because mediad --sim-camera's intrinsics assume it.
   - IMU yaw error (pose_provider.ImuYawError): default ON at the measured magnitudes above; the
     bias sign is random per run.
   - pose_provider.py: GroundTruthProvider (bit-identical to the old reads), OdometryProvider
     (PyOdometry alpha16 + IMU, anchored at the mission's known start), GroundTruthBall.
     - Output speaks mapd's MapState shape plus age_s.
     - Absent past STALE_S 0.1 s ("stale"), never held.
     - A dropout is a link outage.
   - Seams:
     - kick_mission.Mission and goto_point.run_route read pose and ball only through providers.
     - The referee, ball_travel, peak and KICK/SETTLE falls stay ground truth.
     - Absent pose: zero twist; resume when back; POSE_LOST_ABORT_S 5 s -> clean abort.
   - Field (robot/microduck/field.xml + scene_field.xml): boards at +-3.0 m.
     - 0.30 m tall, 12 mm, matte light wood (painted plywood or MDF at arrival).
     - Goal mouths 0.25 m wide with 0.25 m boxes at both ends; both goals parked in the mouths.
     - Geofence = boards inset 0.5 m = (-2.5, 2.5, -2.5, 2.5) = the old ARENA, read back from the model.
     - Render extent pinned as in the goal scene.
