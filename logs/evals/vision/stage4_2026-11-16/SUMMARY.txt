A. GOAL PERCEPTION (scripts/camera_goal.py, `--goal-src camera`)
   - Detect: bright unsaturated blobs, kept only if they are the size a 120 x 20 mm post would be at their floor-ranged base.
     Pair: base-ranged spacing within 80 mm of 230 mm (210 mm opening + one post). Fit: Gauss-Newton on (centre x, y, facing)
     from both posts' columns (sigma 0.5 px) and base/top rows (sigma 1.5 px), the camera's own intrinsics (fx 348 @ 640x360).
   - The field holds a SECOND real goal (the field's own goal at x = -3). The detector reports every goal; the mission is told
     WHICH goal to attack as a coarse assignment (the goal's nominal spot rounded to a 0.25 m grid, gate 0.6 m) -- an instruction
     like a go-to-point waypoint. WHERE the goal is comes only from the camera.
   Ranging vs truth (goal_ranging.txt; duck standing, head neutral, 15 placements per range = 5 bearings x 3 facings):
     | dist | found | fit centre err mm med/max | base-only mm med/max | facing err deg med/max | KICK-HEADING err deg med/max |
     | 0.6  | 13/15 | 5.8 / 13.8 | 10.9 / 30.1 | 0.2 / 1.1 | 0.2 / 0.5 |
     | 0.9  | 15/15 | 6.2 / 7.5  | 10.9 / 13.8 | 0.2 / 0.8 | 0.2 / 0.4 |
     | 1.2  | 15/15 | 6.2 / 12.4 | 11.4 / 18.1 | 0.2 / 1.3 | 0.1 / 0.6 |
     | 1.6  | 15/15 | 7.8 / 14.4 | 12.1 / 20.0 | 0.3 / 6.3 | 0.3 / 0.6 |
     | 2.0  | 15/15 | 7.6 / 21.3 | 17.7 / 34.5 | 0.6 / 7.7 | 0.2 / 0.9 |
     (the 2 misses at 0.6 m: a post outside the frame at the widest bearing). The spacing fit halves the base-only error.
   - Mission: kick heading = ball -> perceived goal centre, both camera-relative (world pose error cancels). The goal is looked at
     head-NEUTRAL: in SCAN (turn until ball AND goal are known) and again at the pre-stance point BEFORE the head-down ball look;
     the heading is refreshed from the fresh ball + fresh goal. --calib v2 aim offsets unchanged (heading source replaced only).
   - Goal not visible at the line-up (defined before building): head-yaw sweep 0 / +0.5 / -0.5 rad, then abort goal_not_found.
     A kick on a stale or absent goal is never taken. In SCAN: goal_not_found after SCAN_MAX_S.
   - In the batteries: |aim error| at swap-in (duck-relative: perceived kick line vs true ball->goal line) median 0.6-0.8 deg,
     max 2.0 deg (300 kicks); heading change at the re-observe median 0.9-1.8 deg.

B. REAL mapd IN THE LOOP (scripts/mapd_shim.py, `--pose mapd`)
   - Unmodified upstream mapd (~/microduck_runtime @ 094d4bb4, 0.21.1, release build). The shim serves robotd (robot.mode ->
     autonomous: the "duck on its own" gate, exercised: [map] enabled = false) and tofd (tof.stream), newline JSON-RPC in
     duck-ipc-proto's format; JSON checked against the real Rust types (RobotState/TofFrame -> StateSample/DepthFrame).
     Fed: odometry (stride_v1) + IMU (with the yaw error) + ToF pose from FK + every ToF frame (15 Hz, 60 ms, all Stage 2 flaws).
   - LOCKSTEP: every input waits for map.status t_ns = its stamp, so a battery is deterministic and mapd's two feed threads cannot
     race. One mapd per battery, map.wipe + 2 s clock gap per run.
   - Into the pose: autod's pattern -- offset = map (-) odom, estimate = offset (+) odometry; lost/silent = keep the last offset.
   - With --pose mapd a mission opens with autod's survey (head yaw 0/+0.6/-0.6/0, 2.4 s still) so mapd has a first keyframe.
   REGIMES (mapd_regimes.txt; 2 laps of a 1 m square, 9 survey stops, driven on mapd; odometry-only with the SAME IMU seed rides
   along on the identical motion; 10 trials each):
     | regime      | odom-only xy mm med/p90/max | WITH mapd med/p90/max | last stop odom -> mapd | corrections/trial |
     | near-boards | 26.2 / 52.6 / 71.8          | 25.7 / 52.0 / 71.8    | 27 -> 24 mm            | 2                 |
     | open field  | 29.7 / 56.8 / 93.5          | 29.7 / 56.8 / 93.5    | 34 -> 34 mm            | 0                 |
   FINDING, plainly: near the boards mapd makes a few accepted corrections and trims ~3 mm; in the open it contributes NOTHING
   (no structure for the 8x8 ToF to match). One board pins one direction only (mapd's own design note).
   In the GoalKick batteries (field centre, boards 2-3 m away): 0 corrections in 300 runs (2-3 keyframes each) -> pose error at
   kick swap-in WITH mapd median 17.1-19.8 mm vs WITHOUT (odom_goalcam/) 17.1-19.6 mm: no contribution, measured.
   Single go-to-point: 0 keyframes (the duck never stands 0.4 s before arriving).
